### About

I design and ship end-to-end products where AI does something genuinely useful — not a chatbot bolted onto a landing page. Most of my work sits at the intersection of **conversational AI, voice interfaces and mobile apps**, from the model prompt all the way down to the database and the deployment.

- Currently building **CronosAI**, an AI voice agent that answers restaurant phone calls and takes bookings and delivery orders.
- Working on a small portfolio of **React Native / Expo** apps around habits, mobility and personal finance.
- I care about products that are useful from the very first user — no network effect required.

---

### Featured work

**[CronosAI](https://github.com/XavierPandele/cronosai-webhook)** — AI voice agent for restaurants  
A phone number that answers itself. Real-time voice conversations powered by **Gemini 2.0 Flash** over **Twilio**, understanding natural speech in 6 languages, confirming table bookings and delivery orders straight into a MySQL backend. Built with a triple-fallback strategy and circuit breakers so a call never dies on the customer, plus structured logging for post-call diagnostics.

`Node.js` · `Google Gemini` · `Twilio Voice` · `Google Cloud TTS` · `MySQL` · `Upstash Redis` · `Vercel`

**Devol-lo** — never lose money on a forgotten return  
Mobile app that stores every online purchase and warns you before the return window closes. Local-first architecture, works offline, useful from day one.

`React Native` · `Expo` · `TypeScript` · `local-first`

**Convoy** — a social layer for group road trips  
Shared trip room where every car appears on the map in real time: distances, separation alerts, "I need to stop" requests and coordinated breaks. It doesn't compete with Google Maps — it sits on top of it.

`React Native` · `Expo` · `real-time geolocation`

**Cinelog** — a film and TV diary that treats series properly  
Track what you watch, rate it, keep a diary and share it. Like Letterboxd, but built for series from the start.

`React Native` · `Expo` · `TMDb API`
